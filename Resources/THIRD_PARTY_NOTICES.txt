Online services used at runtime (not bundled)
==============================================================================
- LRCLIB (https://lrclib.net): community synced-lyrics database. Lyrics remain
  the property of their respective rights holders.
- iTunes Search API (https://itunes.apple.com/search): album lookup and artwork.
- Apple Music API (https://api.music.apple.com): motion artwork, only when you
  provide your own developer token.

macOS system sounds (the "macOS Padlock" lock sound option) are played in place
from /System/Library and are not copied into or distributed with Islet.
